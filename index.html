Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Are kids welcome?

Kids are welcome at the morning ceremony and the evening reception.

What's the weather like in January?

Warm and dry — expect highs around 30°C and lows around 24°C, with little to no rain. Light, breathable clothing is best, with maybe a layer for the evening reception.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes — Malabar Marina Convention Centre has ample on-site parking, plus valet service.

Who should I contact with questions?

Reach out to Larissa or Nihal on whatsapp.

When should I book my flight to India?

We recommend booking by October 2026 (ideal) or November 2026 at the latest. You'll also need to apply for a visa at least 30 days before you travel, so please plan accordingly.

Where should we stay?

We recommend The Raviz Kadavu (~11.5 km / 15 min drive from the venue) or Staydium Bungalow Resort (~16 km / 20 min drive from the venue).
